Questions
Things parents ask us
Is sortd free to use?
Yes — completely free for parents. Always. sortd makes money from camp providers who pay to be listed and featured, not from the parents using the directory.
Do I need to create an account?
No account, no login, no app to download. Just open sortd-ireland.ie on your phone or computer and start searching. That's it.
How do I actually book a camp?
Click "Book this camp" on any listing and you'll go straight to the provider's own booking page. sortd doesn't take the booking — we just help you find the camp. You book directly with the provider, usually online, by email or by phone.
Are all the camps on sortd verified?
Every listing on sortd is researched and added by hand. We check the details directly from the provider's website, Instagram or by contacting them. That said, camp details can change — always confirm with the provider before booking, especially for dates and prices.
My area isn't covered yet — when will it be?
We started with north Dublin and are expanding county by county. Cork, Galway, Limerick and more are coming. The fastest way to get your area covered is to recommend camps to us — we'll add them and let you know when they're live.
I run a camp — how do I get listed?
We'd love to have you. Listing is free and takes about three minutes. Click "list your camp" in the navigation and fill in the details — we'll have you live within 24 hours.
How do I know if places are still available?
Each listing shows a live status — "Live Now" means booking is open, "Waitlist" means it's full but you can still join the queue. We update this regularly, but always check with the provider directly for the most up-to-date availability.
