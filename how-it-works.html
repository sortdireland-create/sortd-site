How sortd works

sortd is a free, parent-curated guide to kids' camps, weekly classes and days out across Dublin, Wicklow, Meath and beyond, with a family calendar built in. Find something, add it to your calendar with school breaks already taken out, and see where your friends' kids are going. No algorithms. No ads. No WhatsApp groups to join.

Four steps
From chaos to a sorted week.

Searching needs no account. Your calendar is free, with just your email. No app to download.

01
Find what they'll love

Search camps and weekly classes by eircode or area, age and day of the week, or browse What's on for days out, shows and free things to do this weekend. Every listing shows the days or weeks it runs, ages, times, cost and where it is, and links straight to the provider to book.

Try: "D13", "Malahide" or "Tuesdays"
02
Add it to your Sortd calendar

Tap "Add to my Sortd calendar" on any class or day out. Pick which child is going and which slot, and it shows every week in term time, until the end of term or the end of the school year. Got a class that isn't on sortd? Add your own.

Free with your email · no password
03
Never get caught out by a day off

Midterms, school holidays and bank holidays are already taken out. Add your school and its inset days and half days, or pick your school from the list and use the dates other parents there have shared. Then send the lot to Google, Apple or Outlook in one tap.

Every school in Ireland · days off shared by parents
04
Plan it with other parents

Send your invite link to a friend or the class WhatsApp group. Once you're connected, you'll see which summer camps each other's kids are going to and whether they're booked, and you can add the same camp in one tap. Costs, pick-ups and leave stay private.

Questions
Things parents ask us
Is sortd free to use?
Yes. Searching, the calendar and planning with friends are all free for parents, always. Providers can list a camp or class for free too; the Partners portal offers optional extra visibility for providers who want it.
Do I need to create an account?
Not to search. Browse camps, classes and days out without signing up. To save things to your Sortd calendar you need a free account: enter your email and we send you a link to log in. There's no password to remember.
What is the Sortd calendar?
Your family's week in one place. Add a class from any class page and it shows every week in term time, with midterms, school holidays and bank holidays already taken out. Add your school's inset days and half days, days out from the monthly guide, and your own classes that aren't on Sortd. In the summer it shows each child's camps week by week.
Can I put it on my phone's calendar?
Yes. Any class can go to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook, or tap "Add all to my phone's calendar" to add everything in one go. School breaks are already taken out, so you won't get reminders for classes that aren't on.
Where do the school days off come from?
Midterms, school holidays and bank holidays follow the standard Department of Education school year. For your own school, add its inset days and half days, or pick your school from the list and use the days off other parents there have already shared.
Who can see my calendar?
Only you. If you connect with a friend through your invite link, they can see your first name, your children's first names and ages, and which summer camps they're going to and whether they're booked. Costs, the people helping with pick-ups, your leave and everything else stay private. Either of you can disconnect at any time. If you share a school day off, only the date goes to other parents at that school, never who added it.
How do I actually book?
Click through on any listing and you'll go straight to the provider's own booking page or contact details. sortd doesn't take the booking; you book directly with the provider, usually online, by email or by phone. Then add it to your Sortd calendar so you don't miss a week.
Are all the listings on sortd verified?
Every camp, class and day out is researched and added by hand. We check the details from the provider's or venue's website, Instagram or by contacting them. Details can change, so always confirm with the provider before booking, especially dates and prices.
I run a camp or class — how do I get listed?
We'd love to have you. Head to sortd-ireland.ie/list. It's free and takes about three minutes.
My area isn't covered yet — when will it be?
We started in north Dublin and are expanding across Leinster, with Wicklow and Meath already live. The fastest way to get your area covered is to email us a recommendation and we'll add it.
Do you have a newsletter?
Yes. Every Thursday we send one short email: new camps and classes, open spots and what's on that weekend. Sign up in the box at the bottom of any page, and unsubscribe in one click. We also post updates on Instagram at @sortd.ireland.
