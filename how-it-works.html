Questions
Things parents ask us
Is sortd free to use?
Yes — it's free for everyone. Free for parents to search and browse, and free for providers to list a camp or class. The Partners portal offers optional extra visibility for providers who want it, but sortd itself is free for everyone.
I run a camp or class — how do I get listed?
We'd love to have you. Head to sortd-ireland.ie/list — it's free and takes about three minutes. If you'd like more visibility or better placement, check out the Partners portal.
Do I need to create an account?
No account, no login, no app to download. Just open sortd-ireland.ie on your phone or computer and start searching. That's it.
Do you have weekly classes too, or just camps?
Both. Camps covers holiday camps for midterms and summer; Weekly classes covers term-time activities like swimming, dance, GAA and more. Use the nav to jump straight to whichever you need, or search from the homepage and pick a type.
How do I actually book?
Click through on any listing and you'll go straight to the provider's own booking page or contact details. sortd doesn't take the booking — we just help you find the camp or class. You book directly with the provider, usually online, by email or by phone.
Are all the listings on sortd verified?
Every listing on sortd is researched and added by hand. We check the details directly from the provider's website, Instagram or by contacting them. That said, details can change — always confirm with the provider before booking, especially for dates and prices.
My area isn't covered yet — when will it be?
We started in north Dublin and are expanding across Leinster — Wicklow and Meath are already live, with Kildare, Louth, Laois, Offaly, Westmeath, Longford, Carlow, Kilkenny and Wexford next. The fastest way to get your area covered is to email us a recommendation and we'll add it.
How do I know if places are still available?
Each listing shows a live status where we have it — "Live Now" means booking is open, "Waitlist" means it's full but you can still join the queue. We update this regularly, but always check with the provider directly for the most up-to-date availability.
Do you have a newsletter?
We've moved to Instagram for updates — it's where new camps and classes, open spots and last-minute changes get posted first. Follow @sortd.ireland to stay in the loop, no inbox required.
