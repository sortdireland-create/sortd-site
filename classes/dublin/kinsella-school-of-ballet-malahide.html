About

RAD ballet curriculum ladder: PrePrimary Ballet (gentle intro through stories/imagery, builds co-ordination and motor skills) → Primary Ballet (age 5-7, RAD syllabus, optional Helix Theatre performances) → Grade 1 & 2 (age 8-9, Class Awards + Helix Theatre performances) → Grade 3+ (age 10+, 2 classes/week encouraged, option to sit RAD exams) → PrePointe (age 14+, 2 classes/week compulsory for students working toward Pointe).
