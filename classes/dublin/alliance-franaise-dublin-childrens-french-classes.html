Timetable

  • TODDLERS 'A Petits Pas' (17-wk terms, ages 2-6): APP2 Fri 4-4:45pm or Sat 9-9:45am (€269/term); APP3 Fri 3:45-4:45pm or Sat 10:15-11:15am (€315); APP4 Fri 2:30-3:30pm or Sat 11:40am-12:40pm (€315); APP5 Fri 2:45-3:45pm or Sat 9-10am (€315).
  • CHILDREN 'Prim'' (17-wk terms, ages 6-12): Prim1 Sat 10:15-11:30am (€395); Prim2-6 & Beginners 7-9/10-12 mostly Sat mornings 9:30-11:30am, some Sat afternoon or Wed DELF options (€535).
  • TEENAGERS (15-wk terms, ages 12-18, 1st-6th Year): mix of Fri 5-7pm and Sat 12:15-2:15pm or 2:30-4:30pm depending on year (€479); online option (3rd & 6th Year) Fri 4:45-6:15pm (€355).
  • BILINGUAL STREAM (separate track for French-speaking families, ages 3-17): Cycle 1 'Les Maternelles' (3-5yo) Saturdays (€315-395); Cycle 2 (6-9yo) Sat 12:15-2:15pm (€535); Cycle 3 (9-13yo) Sat 2:30-4:30pm or Thu 4:45-6:15pm (€395-535); Cycle Perfectionnement (14-17yo) Wed 4:45-6:45pm (€395) or Sat 2:30-4:30pm (€479).

Times are from Alliance Française Dublin (alliance-francaise.ie). Check with them before your first class.
