About

Junior Development Academy for boys and girls from Junior Infants (roughly age 4) up to age 10, no basketball experience required. Runs Sunday mornings during the school year (Sept–April) at the club's IWA court venue in Clontarf. No published fee found; the separate 'Juvenile Basketball 2025/26' page (killester.com/juvenile-2022) confirms the club also runs age-graded teams up to U20, contact info@killester.com for current fees and exact times.
