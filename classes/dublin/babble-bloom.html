About

Babble & Bloom is a 6-week parent-and-baby course designed to support your baby’s communication development. Led by a Speech & Language Therapist, the course introduces simple, evidence-based language facilitation strategies that you can use in everyday routines, play, and interactions with your baby. Throughout the course, you will learn and practise key communication-building techniques, including baby signs, responsive interaction strategies, and ways to support your baby’s understanding and early language skills. Each session combines short, practical teaching with a play-based class where you can immediately put new skills into practice with your child. Suitable for babies and toddlers aged 6–14 months, Babble & Bloom offers a relaxed and supportive environment to connect with your baby, build confidence in supporting their development, and meet other parents at a similar stage. Join us for six weeks of play and practical learning that will help you nurture your baby’s communication skills in everyday moments. Location: Malahide Castle Sports Pavilion
