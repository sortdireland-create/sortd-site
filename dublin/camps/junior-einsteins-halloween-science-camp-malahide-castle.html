About this camp

Hands-on Spooky Science: bubbling vampire blood, plasma balls and flying saucers, oobleck swamp creatures, witches' snot slime, foaming witches' brew eruptions and fossil making. Lab coat, goggles and gloves supplied. Grouped by age (5–8 and 9–11).
