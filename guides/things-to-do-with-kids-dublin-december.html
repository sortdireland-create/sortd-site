Things to do with kids in Dublin this December

I meant to book Santa in October. It's nearly December and what I have is a spreadsheet, so here we are. I found 35 things to do with kids across Dublin in December, plenty of them free, and these are the best 10, sorted by week. Schools close on Tue 22 Dec.

Key dates

  • Tue 1 DecSensory-friendly night at Wild Lights, Dublin Zoo
  • Fri 4, 11 & 18 DecSensory Santa sessions at Newbridge House
  • Sat 19 DecRelaxed 10:30am Snowman show at the National Concert Hall
  • Sat 19 DecThe 3Olympia panto opens
  • Tue 22 DecLast day of school before Christmas (most schools close at 12)

All month

TwinkleTown Smithfield

Smithfield Square · Until Thu 31 Dec · 11am–9pm weekdays, 10am–9pm weekends · All ages · Free entry (skating from €8.75, Santo Panto €5)

Big wheel, funfair and a covered ice rink, free to wander. They run sensory-friendly skating and some ND-friendly Santo Panto shows.

Wild Lights, Dublin Zoo

Dublin Zoo, Phoenix Park · Every night except 24–26 Dec · 5pm–9pm, last entry 7:30pm · All ages · Adults €26.50, children €22, under-3s free

Giant lanterns across 9 zones, theme is Passport to Wonder. There's a sensory-friendly night on Tue 1 Dec.

Wonderlights, Malahide Castle

Malahide Castle & Gardens · Until Tue 29 Dec · 5pm–8:30pm, timed tickets · All ages · From €17.50

A lit-up trail through the castle grounds with an Alpine food village at the end. Weeknights are calmer.

The Magic of Christmas, Airfield Estate

Airfield Estate, Dundrum · Selected dates until Tue 22 Dec · 10am–6:50pm · Little kids (4–7) · €12.50 to €32.50

Santa on a working farm, with cookie decorating, a craft and Santa's real reindeer.

A Christmas Carol, The Ark

The Ark, Temple Bar · Sat & Sun 11am and 2pm until 20 Dec, plus holiday dates to 30 Dec · Ages 7+ · €18.50

Scrooge for children, 45 minutes and properly funny. Relaxed and ISL performances in December.

Sunday family drop-ins, National Gallery

National Gallery of Ireland, Merrion Square · Every Sunday · 11:30am–1:30pm · All ages · Free

An artist runs a weekly drop-in art session in the Shaw Room. Turn up, make something, leave the mess.

1 – 10 December

Tickles and Dottie are Freezin', The Civic Panto

The Civic, Tallaght · From Wed 2 Dec · 6:30pm, matinees 2pm · All ages · €27 (€25 concession), family of 4 €96

A Frozen-ish panto in Tallaght. Cheaper than the city ones and it sells out every year.

11 – 20 December

Sustainable Christmas Craft Market

National Botanic Gardens, Glasnevin · Sat 12 & Sun 13 Dec · from 10am · All ages · Free

Stalls by the glasshouses, mistletoe and seasonal treats. Then let them loose in the gardens.

This Way to Christmas! Featuring The Snowman

National Concert Hall · Fri 18 – Sun 20 Dec · relaxed show Sat 19 Dec 10:30am · All ages · Child €23, adult €30, family €95

Theatre Lovett and a live orchestra playing The Snowman. The Sat 10:30am show is relaxed, and disabled people and carers get 50% off.

Mission Santa: Crack the Christmas Code

Draíocht, Blanchardstown · Fri 18 Dec · 4pm and 5:30pm (50 mins) · Little kids (4–7) · Children €5, adults €8

A sing-along show where the audience finds Santa's missing code. €5 a child, which is basically free.

FAQ

What's free to do with kids in Dublin in December?

TwinkleTown in Smithfield and St Patrick's Park is free to walk around, and so are the National Gallery's Sunday family drop-ins, the Dead Zoo Lab at Collins Barracks, the Sustainable Christmas Craft Market at the Botanic Gardens (Sat 12 and Sun 13 Dec) and the free family events in local libraries. IMMA and St Patrick's Cathedral also run free Christmas family workshops, listed on Sortd.

What can we do with kids in Dublin on a rainy day in December?

The Ark in Temple Bar, the National Gallery, the Dead Zoo Lab and EPIC are all indoors. So are the pantos at the Gaiety, The Helix, The Civic and 3Olympia, and The Snowman at the National Concert Hall (18 to 20 Dec).

When do schools close for Christmas 2026?

Under the standardised school calendar, the last day of term is Tuesday 22 December 2026 and schools reopen on Wednesday 6 January 2027. Check your own school's calendar, as some close at midday on the 22nd.

Is there a bank holiday on Monday 28 December?

Christmas Day (Fri 25 Dec) and St Stephen's Day (Sat 26 Dec) are the public holidays. Because St Stephen's Day falls on a Saturday, many employers give Monday 28 December off instead, but it isn't automatic, so check with yours.
