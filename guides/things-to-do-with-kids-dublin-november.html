The November guide · 2026

Things to do with kids in Dublin this November

November has no bank holiday and a lot of dark afternoons. It also has a free book festival day, a whole week of science and the start of the light trails. Here are the best bits, sorted by week.

Key dates

  • No bank holidayThe next one is Christmas Day. Midterm finished on Fri 30 Oct.
  • Wed 4 – Sun 8 NovDublin Book Festival, with a free family day on Sat 7 Nov
  • Sat 7 – Sun 15 NovNorth Dublin Science Festival, free events
  • Sun 8 – Sun 15 NovScience Week across Ireland
  • Thu 12 NovWild Lights at Dublin Zoo and Wonderlights at Malahide Castle open
  • Sat 14 NovElf Town opens at the RDS
  • Fri 20 NovTwinkleTown opens in Smithfield

All month

The Dead Zoo Lab, Collins Barracks

Dublin 7 · Tue–Sat 10am–5pm, Sun–Mon 1pm–5pm · All ages · Free

The Natural History Museum's animals have a temporary home while Merrion Street is closed. Free, indoors, and the best wet-day plan in the city.

Sunday family drop-ins, National Gallery

Merrion Square · Sundays 11:30am–1:30pm · All ages · Free

Rock up, grab the materials and make something. No booking needed.

1–10 November

Dublin Book Festival family day

Royal Irish Academy of Music, Westland Row · Sat 7 Nov from 11am · All ages · Free

A drop-in treasure hunt, craft station and children's book swap. Bring a book they've outgrown.

Babies @ The Ark: Excitedly Resting

Temple Bar · Until Sat 14 Nov, Wed–Sat · Ages 0 to 2 · Free

A soft, sensory textile space for babies to lie in, crawl under and explore. No booking.

North Dublin Science Festival

DCU, The Helix and Deaf Village Ireland · Sat 7 – Sun 15 Nov · All ages · Free

Water-themed science all week, including a drop-in STEM space in Irish Sign Language (9–13 Nov) and swimming films projected on The Helix (Wed 11 Nov, 5–8pm).

11–20 November

Wild Lights, Dublin Zoo

Phoenix Park · From Thu 12 Nov, 5pm–9pm · All ages · Adults €26.50, children €22, under-3s free

Giant lanterns across 9 zones themed on celebrations from around the world. There's a sensory-friendly night on Tue 1 Dec. Weekends sell out first.

Wonderlights, Malahide Castle

Malahide · From Thu 12 Nov, 5pm–8:30pm · All ages · €17.50

An illuminated trail through the castle grounds. Wrap up and go early in the run for the quieter nights.

Elf Town, RDS

Ballsbridge · From Sat 14 Nov · All ages · Santa visit from €18 per child

A 90-minute Christmas journey that ends with a private visit with Santa. A shorter Santa-only visit is cheaper.

TwinkleTown, Smithfield

Smithfield Square · From Fri 20 Nov, 11am–9pm weekdays, 10am–9pm weekends · All ages · Free entry

A big wheel, funfair, ice rink and a 20-minute Santo Panto at weekends (€5). Sensory skating from Sat 28 Nov at 11am, and the 12pm weekend panto is ND-friendly.

21–30 November

A Christmas Carol, The Ark

Temple Bar · From Wed 25 Nov, Sat & Sun 11am and 2pm · Ages 7+ · €18.50

A 45-minute Scrooge made for children. Relaxed and Irish Sign Language performances run in December.

Swords Castle Christmas Fair

Swords · Fri 27 Nov 3pm–7pm, Sat 28 & Sun 29 Nov 12pm–6pm (and 5–6 Dec) · All ages · Free

A proper castle-courtyard Christmas market, run by Fingal County Council. Hours are provisional, so check before you go.

A Christmas to Remember, Newbridge House

Donabate · Weekends from Sat 28 Nov, plus 21–22 Dec · All ages · Child €28.50, adult €15.50

Hot chocolate, Mrs Claus making puddings, a walk through the farmyard and Santa by the fire.

FAQ

What's free to do with kids in Dublin in November?

The Dead Zoo Lab at Collins Barracks, the National Gallery's Sunday family drop-ins, the Dublin Book Festival family day (Sat 7 Nov), Babies @ The Ark and the North Dublin Science Festival. TwinkleTown and the Swords Castle Christmas Fair are free to walk around.

What can we do with kids in Dublin on a rainy day?

Museums and galleries are the safe bet: the Dead Zoo Lab, the National Gallery and The Ark in Temple Bar. Library storytimes also run weekly across the city.

When is the next school midterm?

The October midterm was 26–30 Oct 2026. The Christmas holidays start in late December. Check your own school's calendar, as some primary schools have extra closure days this year for curriculum training.
