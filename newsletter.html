Why I send it
I needed this, so I built it.

I'm a working mum to Ella and Finn. When the school holidays came around, information about camps was scattered everywhere: Instagram, WhatsApp groups, websites that hadn't been updated since 2022. If you weren't in the right group, you missed out.

There was no one place to see it all, so I built one, on a Monday night. Parents started sharing it with other parents, and providers started asking to be added. We're growing county by county.

The Thursday email is the same idea, delivered to you: I do the hunting, you get the short version.
